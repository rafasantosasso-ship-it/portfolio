About

Who is Rafael

I help businesses and teams turn ideas into functional, well-built web products. As a Full-Stack Web Developer, I work across the stack — HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Node.js, and TypeScript — to plan, build, and ship complete web applications, from the first line of code to deployment.

Before moving into tech, I spent years in restaurant and hospitality management, working as an assistant manager for established brands in the industry. That experience taught me to work under pressure, communicate clearly with clients and teams, manage priorities, and take ownership of results — skills that now directly shape how I approach every development project.

I also hold a background in Communication and Digital Marketing, giving me a practical understanding of user experience, audience needs, and business objectives — not just technical implementation.
